For builders

Any AI agent can run the same check before it acts on research:

  • From a contract: BondedClaims.isCleared(id). The trading desk here reverts any trade backed by a claim that isn't cleared.
  • Over HTTP: POST /api/verify with a ticker, a figure and the sentence. Or use .
  • As an MCP tool: verify_claim, for agents that use Model Context Protocol.
  • Verdicts come from Chainlink: a CRE workflow fetches the filing from SEC EDGAR, runs the check on every node and writes the verdict through the Chainlink forwarder. The contract accepts no other source.
  • Audit a trade: python -m vouchrh.journal verify feed.json --rpc … re-checks every journal entry against the chain and EDGAR.
How it works, in full

  1. Bond. An analyst deposits USDG into the contract before it may publish.

  2. Claim. Each claim quotes the company's SEC filing and puts part of the bond at stake. The words stay off-chain; their hash goes on-chain, so a claim cannot be edited after it is checked.

  3. Check. The check is deterministic: a whole sentence of the filing, every figure, period, date and direction as filed. Anyone can also challenge within the window.

  4. Settle. A Chainlink CRE workflow checks the claim against the filing and writes the verdict. A wrong claim loses its stake to a pool that backs readers (half to a challenger, if someone challenged first). A right one keeps it.

  5. Gate. Contracts can ask whether a claim is cleared, so an agent can only trade on research that survived.
