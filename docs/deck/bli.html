The legal gap

Research rules assume a person. AI drafts research now, and cannot certify a belief.

Regulation AC · 17 CFR 242.501(a)(1)a certification "attesting that all of the views expressed in the research report accurately reflect the research analyst's personal views". The analyst: "any natural person…" (242.500)
FINRA Rule 2241(c)(1)(A)procedures "reasonably designed to ensure that purported facts in its research reports are based on reliable information"
FINRA Regulatory Notice 24-09FINRA's rules "are intended to be technology neutral" and "continue to apply when member firms use Gen AI"
FINRA 2026 oversight report · AI agentsconsider "how to track agent actions and decisions; or how to establish guardrails"

How it works

A record of what was said, what the filing says, and what was done about it.

01

Stake

An analyst, human or AI, posts one figure, the 10-Q sentence behind it and a USDG stake. The exact words go on-chain.

02

Check

A Chainlink CRE workflow fetches the filing from SEC EDGAR on every node and runs the check. No verifier key.

03

Pay

Wrong: the stake goes to a pool that backs readers, in the same transaction as the verdict. Right: it comes back.

04

Gate

A trading agent cannot cite research that failed. The trade reverts, naming the claim.

05

Journal

Each trade keeps the claims it cites, the verdict on each and the agent's reasoning, hashed on-chain.

For lawyers

What the record proves, and what it does not.

It proves

  • the exact words of each claim, who staked on them, when
  • which filing bytes the verdict was checked against, and by which workflow
  • that a trade cited only claims that had cleared
  • that its stated reasons were fixed when it was made

It does not prove

  • that a view was honestly held (Reg AC's certification)
  • a reasonable basis for a rating or target (2241(c)(1)(B))
  • that the filing is true, or the claim complete
  • who is behind a wallet, or Rule 17a-4 compliance
