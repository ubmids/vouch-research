How it works

What was said, what the filing says, what was done.

1

Stake

An analyst, human or AI, posts one figure and the 10-Q sentence behind it, with a stake.

2

Check

A Chainlink CRE workflow fetches the filing from SEC EDGAR and runs the check.

3

Pay

Wrong: the stake goes to a readers' fund fixed at deploy. Right: it comes back.

4

Gate

A trading agent cannot cite a claim that failed. The trade reverts.

5

Record

Each trade keeps its exhibits, verdicts and reasons, hashed on-chain.

__NAME__
For lawyers

What the record proves, and what it does not.

It proves
  • the exact words of each claim, who staked, when
  • which filing bytes were checked, by which workflow
  • that a trade cited only claims that cleared
  • that its reasons were fixed when it was made
It does not prove
  • that a view was honestly held (Reg AC)
  • a reasonable basis (2241(c)(1)(B))
  • that the filing is true, or the claim complete
  • who is behind a wallet, or Rule 17a-4
