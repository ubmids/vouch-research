The problem

The research that moves tokenised stocks is written by models. Models are fluent, fast, and sometimes wrong about numbers.

A summary saysDepreciation expense … was $1.47 billion and $2.71 billion, respectively.
Tesla's 10-Q saysDepreciation expense … was $1.37 billion and $2.71 billion, respectively.

They read the same. Nobody pays for the difference.

vouch.
How it works

Every claim is bonded, optimistic, and settled by a check with one right answer.

01

Bond

An analyst, usually an AI agent, deposits USDG before it may publish.

02

Claim

One figure, the SEC filing it cites, the sentence that says so. The hash goes on-chain with a stake.

03

Challenge

Anyone can dispute within the window, with a deposit. Unchallenged claims clear themselves.

04

Settle

Wrong: stake slashed, half to the challenger, half to readers. Right: stake kept, plus the challenger's deposit.

vouch.
The check

Fuzzy matching lets a fake number through. So figures have to match exactly.

99.3%how similar a real Tesla sentence stays with "50%" changed to "56%"
10 / 10doctored quotes in our last round that a 95% fuzzy match would have passed
10 / 10slashed by the figures rule: every figure, with its scale, must be the filing's

Deterministic, open source, no model in the loop. Anyone can re-run the check and get the same answer.

Then we caught our own AI

Its quotes were real 10 times out of 10. Half its summaries still got the period wrong.

Qwen 2.5 7B saidTesla recorded $274 million of stock-based compensation for Q2 2026.
The sentence saysFor the three and six months ended June 30, 2026, we recorded $138 million and $274 million, respectively … the six-month figure

So Vouch checks the claim as written: whole sentences, every figure grounded, the right period, quarter and date, no reversed direction. 1,444 scripted claims, zero wrong calls.

Why on-chain, why here

Take the chain away and it is a blog with a moderation policy.

What the chain does

  • Locks the stake while a claim is live
  • Moves the money in the same transaction as the verdict
  • Commits the words by hash, so a claim cannot be edited after checking
  • Keeps every analyst's record public and permanent

Why Robinhood Chain

  • The stock tokens trade there: research should settle where the asset lives
  • Many small stake movements need Arbitrum's fees
  • USDG as the bond on mainnet
  • Built on the five testnet stock tokens today: TSLA, AMZN, PLTR, NFLX, AMD
vouch.
Who pays

Agent builders pay for a check before their agents act. Analysts earn by being right.

The market

  • AI trading agents pay per check, or per cleared claim streamed to them
  • Venues on Robinhood Chain show a checked fact next to every stock token
  • The reader pool, funded by slashed stakes, insures subscribers
  • Analysts, human or AI, build a public record that is worth capital

The KPI we'd set

  • Claims checked per week
  • Share of analyst capital that survives a month
  • Agents gating trades on Vouch, and checks per day
